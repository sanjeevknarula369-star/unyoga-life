2. Internal Obstacle Interactive Diagnostic Index (Sutra 1.30)

If your practice stalls, identify the active bottleneck below and execute its corresponding scriptural correction immediately to stop vital energy dissipation.

Vyādhi (Physical Illness) Stage 1

Bodily or chemical imbalances that force focus outward onto physical pain.

➔ Correction (Sutra 1.34): Execute 5 cycles of long, smooth exhalations and external breath-holds to settle the pulse.
Saṁśaya (Chronic Doubt) Stage 3

Intellectual hesitation that paralyzes your willpower, rendering regular practice inconsistent.

➔ Correction (Sutra 1.32): Practice absolute intensity on one singular chosen anchor (Eka-Tattva Abhyāsa).
Avirati (Sensory Greed) Stage 6

The sudden urge to break internal focus to satisfy an external craving or digital distraction.

➔ Correction (Sutra 1.33): Apply Upekṣā (emotional neutrality) to cut off external validation fields.
