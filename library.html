The Ojas Core Architecture

A pristine scriptural manual analyzing the exact internal mechanics of the mind-stuff. Use these foundational formulas to study variations in your baseline consciousness and plug energetic leaks.

Samadhi Pada Sutra 1.2
योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः॥२॥
Yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ
Authentic Translation
"Yoga is the intentional stilling of the changing states, fluctuations, and whirlpools of the mind-stuff."
The Ojas & Consciousness Mechanics

Your total vital force is spent when the mind-stuff (Citta) spins into chaotic thought currents (Vṛttis). True internal preservation begins when you navigate your focus out of the lower fields:

  • Kṣipta & Mūḍha: Scattered anxiety or heavy lethargy that rapidly drains energy.
  • Ekāgra & Nirodha: Single-pointed stillness where raw mental currents collapse back into calm water, completely sealing your energy reserves.

Samadhi Pada Sutra 1.4
वृत्तिसारूप्यमितरत्र॥४॥
Vṛtti sārūpyam itaratra
Authentic Translation
"At other times—when the mind-stuff is not stilled—the true consciousness mistakenly identifies with the changing forms of its passing thoughts."
The Ojas & Consciousness Mechanics

This is the primary leak in human psychology. When a wave of exhaustion or stress arises in your field, Sārūpyam tricks you into thinking, "I am depleted." The moment you identify with the passing movie of the mind, you burn through your deep vitality. Yoga breaks this loop, teaching you to witness the wave instead of becoming it.

Samadhi Pada Sutra 1.14
स तु दीर्घकालनैरन्तर्यसत्कारासेवितो दृढभूमिः॥१४॥
Sa tu dīrgha-kāla-nairantarya-satkārāsevi-to dṛḍha-bhūmiḥ
Authentic Translation
"And that practice becomes a firmly established, unshakable foundation only when it is sustained for a long period of time, without daily gaps, and maintained with absolute sincerity and deep devotion."
The Ojas & Consciousness Mechanics

You cannot build spiritual stamina with fragmented effort. Patanjali outlines a strict timeline protocol: Dīrgha-Kāla (a long, patient horizon), Nairantarya (unbroken daily continuity to seal regular gaps), and Satkāra (loving reverence). Meeting these conditions grants you an unshakable state of being (Dṛḍha-Bhūmiḥ).

Samadhi Pada Sutra 1.32
तत्प्रतिषेधार्थमेकतत्त्वाभ्यासः॥३२॥
Tat-pratiṣedhārtham ekatattvābhyāsaḥ
Authentic Translation
"To completely counteract and neutralize these nine obstacles and their symptoms, one must practice intense concentration on a single principle or unwavering object of focus."
The Ojas & Consciousness Mechanics

When the mind fragments across multiple problems, it scatters internal energy. Ekatattva-Abhyāsa brings the intellect back to a single boundary. Staying completely anchored to one target collapses secondary thinking patterns and consolidates your deep nervous system reserves.
